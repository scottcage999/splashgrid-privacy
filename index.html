Splash Grid Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Splash Grid ("the game") is published by Slatt Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location, and we do not run our own servers that store your personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, settings, streak, and badges) is saved locally on your device. It is not sent to us. If you clear the game's data or uninstall it, this progress is removed.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type, and how you interact with ads, in order to show ads and measure their performance. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game shows a consent form so you can choose how your data is used. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Notifications

With your permission, the game can show one local reminder per day on your device (for example about your daily streak). These reminders are created on your device and do not use any personal information. You can turn them off in the game's help menu or in your Android settings.

Sharing

If you tap "Share result", the game gives a short text to your phone's share menu. It is sent only to the app and person you choose. We do not receive it.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (such as removing ads, coin packs and themes) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so that we can unlock the item.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date above.
